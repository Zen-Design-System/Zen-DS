In Jesse James Garrett’s five planes, the interface is only the top layer of the experience.

- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

  1. (1)UI — color, type, layout, components, states and motion.
  2. (2)UX — needs, flows, structure, and how it feels to use.
  3. (3)The catch — if a lower plane is wrong, no polish on top can save it.
